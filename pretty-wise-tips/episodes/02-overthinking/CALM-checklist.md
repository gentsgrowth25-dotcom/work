# CALM — Your 3-question reset

Pause before rereading the chat. Take a slow, comfortable breath, then ask:

1. **What do I actually know?** Write only what you observed: “There is no reply yet.”
2. **What am I assuming?** Name the story your mind added: “I’m assuming he has lost interest.” A guess is not a fact.
3. **What do I need right now?** Choose one grounded action: drink water, journal, return to a task, or connect with a friend. If inconsistent communication is a recurring pattern, calmly ask for clarity about your needs and expectations.

A message you can adapt: “I value consistent communication. What are you looking for?”

Your peace matters too.
