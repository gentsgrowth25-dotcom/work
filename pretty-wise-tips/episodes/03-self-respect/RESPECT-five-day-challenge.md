# RESPECT — A free five-day self-respect challenge

A small practice each day, from Pretty Wise Tips.

## Day 1 — Say less, kindly

Notice one low-stakes moment when you feel you must justify every detail. Try a short, respectful response: “I can’t today, sorry.” Add practical information when it is needed, without treating a long explanation as the price of saying no.

Reflect: What did I expect would happen? What actually happened?

## Day 2 — Put your goal on the calendar

Choose one personal goal and reserve a realistic 20–60 minute block for it. If someone asks for that time, offer another time if you want to. Use your reserved block to take one concrete step.

Reflect: Did I treat my own time as valuable?

## Day 3 — Notice guilt without treating it as a verdict

After a reasonable decision, write down what you feel and what you actually did. Ask: Did I break an agreement or cause avoidable harm? Or am I uncomfortable with someone being disappointed? Guilt alone does not settle that question. Repair actual harm when appropriate.

Reflect: What evidence helps me assess this fairly?

## Day 4 — Practice one reasonable boundary

Pick a safe, ordinary situation. Say what you can and cannot do, calmly and specifically: “I can help tomorrow, not tonight.” Listen to relevant information and keep the boundary proportionate to the situation.

Reflect: Can I be considerate without automatically saying yes?

## Day 5 — Reflect with compassion

Write one thing you practiced, one thing you would adjust, and one boundary or goal you want to keep. Progress can include discomfort, mistakes, repair, and trying again.

Reflect: What would one more week of this practice look like?

Self-respect takes practice.

— Pretty Wise Tips
