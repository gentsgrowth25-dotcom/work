### SIGN 1 — STOP OVEREXPLAINING

Narration:
"One: You stop overexplaining. Saying 'I can't today' can be enough."

Animation:
Show the woman typing a long message filled with explanations.

The message grows increasingly long.

She pauses, deletes the unnecessary text, and replaces it with a respectful, concise response:

"I can't today, sorry!"

She looks noticeably relieved.

On-screen text:
"1. STOP OVEREXPLAINING"

Use a smooth camera zoom toward the final message.

Show the difference between a cluttered calendar and one with intentional personal time.

On-screen text:

Keep the scene educational and visually easy to understand.

Narration:
"Three: You set a reasonable boundary, even when guilt appears. That discomfort doesn't necessarily mean you're wrong. Sometimes, you're simply practicing self-respect."

Animation:
Show the woman respectfully declining an unreasonable request.
