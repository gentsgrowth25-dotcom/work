# WORTH: your free five-day confidence challenge

**Pretty Wise Tips · Episode 07**

Practice self-respect with one small action each day. Choose an ordinary, low-pressure situation and give yourself about five minutes. You can repeat a day or take longer; this is practice, not a test of your worth.

## Day 1 — Let a compliment land

When someone offers sincere praise, pause and say **“Thank you.”** Resist adding a line that dismisses your effort, such as “It was nothing.” You can still acknowledge someone else's contribution without erasing your own.

Try: “Thank you. I put a lot of care into it.”

Reflect: What did they appreciate? What effort or skill can I acknowledge in myself?

## Day 2 — Ask a reasonable question

Choose one clear, respectful question you would normally soften with an unnecessary apology. Ask it directly, while respecting the other person's time.

Try: “Could you clarify the deadline?” or “Is now a good time for a quick question?”

Reflect: Did my question need an apology, or simply a polite opening? A real mistake still deserves an appropriate apology.

## Day 3 — Name one fair boundary

Identify a genuine commitment, capacity limit or need. Consider your responsibilities and the impact on others, then choose a reasonable boundary for a non-urgent request.

Try: “I already have plans tonight, so I can't help this time.” If it works for you, offer an alternative you can actually keep: “I can look at it tomorrow.”

Reflect: What makes this limit fair? Am I protecting a real need while communicating respectfully?

## Day 4 — Separate a feeling from a verdict

If guilt or discomfort appears after a fair decision, name the feeling before changing your answer. Ask what the facts show: Did I break a commitment, cause avoidable harm or overlook an important responsibility? Listen to specific, legitimate feedback. Someone's disappointment alone does not settle whether your decision was wrong.

Try: “I understand you're disappointed. I still can't today.” If the facts show that you owe a repair, acknowledge it and adjust fairly.

Reflect: What am I feeling? What evidence supports my decision, and what feedback should I consider?

## Day 5 — Notice progress and choose your next step

Review the previous four days. Write down one moment when you accepted praise, spoke clearly or kept a fair boundary. Notice what was difficult without turning it into a judgment about yourself.

Try: “One thing I handled with self-respect was ____. Next week I will practice ____.”

Reflect: Which action helped me stay both kind and clear? What is one small action I want to repeat?

## A simple daily check-in

- Today I practiced: ____
- What I noticed: ____
- One fair next step: ____

Confidence can grow through practice, and uncomfortable feelings may still show up. The goal is thoughtful, respectful choices, not perfect confidence or saying no to every request.
