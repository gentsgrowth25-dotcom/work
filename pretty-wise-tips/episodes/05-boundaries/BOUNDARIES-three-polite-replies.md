# BOUNDARIES — Three polite ways to say no

From Pretty Wise Tips.

1. **“I can’t this time.”**
2. **“Thanks for thinking of me, but I won’t be able to.”**
3. **“I don’t have the capacity to take that on.”**

Choose words that fit the situation. Be respectful and clear. A short answer can be enough; add practical information when it is relevant. Someone else's disappointment alone does not establish that you have done something wrong. Consider commitments you have made and repair actual harm when appropriate.

You can be kind and still say no.
