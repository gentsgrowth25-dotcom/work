## Visual rig and lesson

`GymMaya.tsx` reuses Maya's brunette hair, skin tone and expressive face, with purple-and-white gym clothes. Its upper arm and forearm have fixed lengths. The curl is generated by linked shoulder, elbow and wrist coordinates; the controlled version keeps the elbow steady, while the exaggerated example adds torso sway and elbow travel. The recovery scene specifically compares repeated demanding sessions for the same muscles with a more varied schedule. It does not label consecutive exercise days as inherently harmful.
