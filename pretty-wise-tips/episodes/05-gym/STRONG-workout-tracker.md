Record the load and reps you actually completed with controlled technique. If it helps, log each set on its own row. Note changes in effort, rest, technique, or range of motion so comparisons have context.

An illustration, not a weekly target:

- Week 1: Dumbbell curl, 5 kg × 8 reps.
- Week 2: Dumbbell curl, 5 kg × 9 reps.

Progress can vary. Every workout does not need to improve, and progress is not always weekly or linear. Recovery needs depend on the muscles trained, effort, workload, and the individual; exercising on consecutive days is not automatically a problem.
