This is mandatory. Previous videos drifted away from our colors.

- Primary accent: Our original PURPLE-MAUVE brand color.

## 4. RETENTION REQUIREMENTS

Apply the principles from our five previously analyzed viral scripting reference videos:

- Start with a specific, relatable problem rather than a generic introductory question.
- Create an information gap by promising that the third tip combines the earlier lessons.
- Deliver practical advice throughout.
- Demonstrate each tip with animated examples.
- Place a clear comment CTA around the middle.
- Make the final tip visually satisfying and more comprehensive than the earlier tips.
- Resolve the opening curiosity promise.
- Show what the voiceover means instead of displaying full sentences.

For this episode, the primary retention device is the transformation of an ordinary fruit bowl into a balanced breakfast.

The viewer should understand the practical lesson even when watching without sound.
