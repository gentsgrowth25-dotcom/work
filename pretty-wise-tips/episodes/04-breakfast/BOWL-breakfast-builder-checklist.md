# BOWL — Your breakfast-builder checklist

A flexible guide from Pretty Wise Tips.

- **Include protein:** Greek yogurt or eggs are two options. Choose foods that suit your preferences and dietary needs.
- **Include fiber:** Try oats or other whole grains, alongside fruit you enjoy.
- **Include healthy fats:** Nuts or seeds are convenient options when suitable for you.
- **Combine what you enjoy:** One example is yogurt, oats, berries and nuts. Adjust portions to your appetite and needs.

Fruit can be a nutritious part of breakfast. If a fruit-only breakfast leaves you hungry, pairing it with other foods may make the meal more satisfying. No breakfast guarantees a particular fullness time for everyone.

Balance, not restriction.

— Pretty Wise Tips
