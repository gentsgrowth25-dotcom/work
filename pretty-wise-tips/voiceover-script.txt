Three days. No reply. But he watched every story.
Maya opened the chat and almost typed, ‘Did I do something wrong?’
Then she noticed the pattern: canceled plans, late-night texts, and promises that never turned into effort.
Suddenly, the problem wasn't that she needed to try harder. It was that she kept making excuses for inconsistency.
So she stopped waiting. She called her friends, worked on her goals, and made plans she actually enjoyed.
When his message finally appeared—‘Miss me?’—she didn't have to prove anything.
She had learned one thing: attention feels exciting. Consistency feels safe. Choose the difference.
