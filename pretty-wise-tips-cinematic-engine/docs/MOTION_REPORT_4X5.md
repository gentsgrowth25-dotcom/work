# Actual motion in the 4:5 pilot

The final film uses **14 distinct authored cel-style illustrations** at 1080 × 1350, 30 FPS. Character drawings are held poses. The code adds restrained camera pans/pushes/pulls, clean cuts, two eight-frame dissolves, timed caption entrances/word emphasis and optional subtle analog finish.

One genuine independent illustrated effect is animated: `public/props/4x5/tea-steam.png`, a transparent generated steam overlay in **02B, the teacup detail**. Its registered foreground layer rises from y = −20 px to −37 px while fading from opacity 0 to 0.6 and back to 0. The cup, hand and character drawing remain held. This is independent prop/effect motion, not character acting or an articulated tea-lifting action.

The film does **not** contain independent blinking, lip sync, head turns, body breathing, walking cycles, signing strokes or hand/hair animation. The necklace, street, signing and final portraits are separately authored still poses connected through camera movement and editing. No face, wrist or body is warped to imply an action unavailable in the assets. There is no character-depth parallax from a flattened scene.

The five-second visual test cuts between the actual pearl detail and mirror portrait, with camera movement and synchronized opening captions. Its passed review is bound to both original test PNGs and the bundled `review/pretty-wise-tips-vintage-test-4x5.mp4` hash.

Future true character movement requires compatible registered parts, alternate action drawings or eye/expression cels. The engine retains those controls, but their availability is not a claim that they were used in this film.
