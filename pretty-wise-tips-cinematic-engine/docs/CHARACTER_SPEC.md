## Identity and costume lock

Preserve one mature glamorous adult woman with warm honey-caramel brown skin; elegant almond eyes and arched brows; a simplified angular nose; softly shaped red lips; refined cheeks; and the tiny beauty mark below the outer eye on the viewer-left side in the canonical frontal view. Keep that mark on the same anatomical side when the head angle changes; a true mirror reflection reverses it naturally. Do not duplicate it on both sides.

Her hair is deep chestnut/burgundy-brown: a large sculpted vintage wave and curled forelock with long thick polished waves. Broad flat highlights and the distinctive front curl establish her silhouette. Preserve the same face geometry, hair volume and line/shadow construction in every shot.

The wardrobe is locked across the episode: **deep crimson fitted midi dress, long sleeves, draped waist, pearl studs, short pearl necklace, understated pearl bracelet and matching red pumps**. Lighting may warm or cool those colors; it must not introduce a different dress, neckline, hairstyle or accessory set. Do not replace the lead with an older repository character or vary her outfit for the office.

Her performance is adult, serene, poised, thoughtful, warm and financially self-assured. Use quiet confidence rather than exaggerated arrogance, childish expressions or hypersexualized posing.

## Drawing language

Use original traditional 1990s Western television cel-animation drawing: confident dark umber/black contours; attractive angular anatomy; broad flat color; one or two hard-edged cel-shadow shapes per major surface; limited broad highlights. No glossy airbrushing, 3D volume, modern anime eyes, vector infographic anatomy or photoreal skin.

Backgrounds are hand-painted architectural designs with coherent perspective and restrained surface marks. Environment colors may include burgundy, muted olive, brass, ochre, cream, warm stone and dusty sky blue. Do not recolor the world into a modern lavender presentation. Fine analog grain and slight broadcast softness are optional finishing passes; preview the underlying illustration without them.

## Native raster handoff

Master composition: **1080 × 1350, 4:5, 30 FPS**. Generate/art-direct every scene natively for 4:5; 2160 × 2700 masters are preferable when detailed cropping is needed. Do not stretch, squeeze or blindly crop a 9:16 illustration into this frame. Keep essential faces and action in the native frame, with painted bleed for camera travel of roughly 3–7% per shot.

Mandatory complete shot files are PNG, sRGB, without captions, watermarks, scene labels or guides. Optional independent layers may be transparent PNG or lossless WebP. All layers in one shot must share the exact full-frame canvas, origin, scale and registration. Provide clean fill behind removed parts; preserve alpha without a white/dark fringe.

Caption placement is around 68–78% of the 1350-pixel frame height, adjusted per shot to avoid faces, hands, documents, tea and jewelry. Safe margins are 95 px top, 190 px bottom and 80 px left/right. Do not bake caption-space markers into the art.
